# Profile Builder: User guide

Brief id: `3e540c9c-0f6b-43ed-b4f6-aabf69c1c037`

Create your professional online presence effortlessly.

## How to open it

Double-click `phase3_step5_mvp.html`. It works offline in any browser, on a computer, phone or tablet.

## Screens

- **Browse**: Start here: see what is available or how things stand.
- **Add a listing**: Fill in the form and press Save.
- **My listings**: See what you saved; remove anything you no longer need.
- **Feedback**: Tell the team what to improve. It is saved on this device only.

## What you fill in

- Name (required)
- Email Address (required)
- Phone Number
- CV Template (required): Template A, Template B, Template C
- Portfolio Template (required): Notion Portfolio A, Notion Portfolio B, Notion Portfolio C

## Your data

Everything stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there are no real payments (MOCKED). Clearing the browser's data removes it.

## Not in this MVP (on purpose)

- Real-time collaboration features
- Advanced customization options beyond template selection
