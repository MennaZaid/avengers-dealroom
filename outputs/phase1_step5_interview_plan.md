**Who:** Everyone
**How many:** 5-10 people from the target group, one at a time, 15-30 minutes each

**Where to find them:**
- People posting about this problem on news.ycombinator.com (where the pain quotes came from)
- Groups and communities where the target customers already meet (online and local)
- Introductions from each person you interview (ask at the end)


## Questions (en)

1. Tell me about the last time you ran into this: "People want to chat with AI". What happened?
2. What did you do about it? What have you tried so far?
3. What did that cost you, in money or hours?
4. How do you handle it today? Which tools, apps or people do you use?
5. Walk me through the last time this happened. What did you do?
6. When did you last pay for something to deal with this? What was it?
7. Who else is involved when you deal with this?
8. Who else should I talk to about this?

Avoid:
- Would you use an app that ...?
- How much would you pay for ...?
- Do you like this idea?
- Pitching your product before they describe the problem
