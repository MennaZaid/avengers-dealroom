 | Company overview
 | REE puts a button on the court wall and a camera above it. Players press it after a good point and the last 30 seconds land
 | in the REE app before they leave the court. REE sells to the court operator and reaches every player through them, building

 | football and multi-sport clubs, and streams leagues for Smashi across MENA.
