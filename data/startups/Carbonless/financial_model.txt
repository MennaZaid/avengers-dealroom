### sheet Summary
Carbonless
The first MENA onboard carbon capture (OCCS) startup
Sector | Climate tech / Maritime decarbonisation
Headquarters | Mansoura & Cairo, Egypt
Founded / started operating | Project started Nov 2022 (first funding 2023); company incorporated 2026
Current stage | Pre-seed; raising USD 4M in three milestone-based tranches
Business model | Sale or lease of SENU onboard capture systems + maintenance contracts + sale of captured CO2 (with cash-back to shipowners)
Company overview
Carbonless builds SENU, an onboard carbon capture and storage system (scrubbing → capture → regeneration → liquid storage)
that lets shipowners keep conventional fuels while complying with EU ETS, FuelEU and IMO net-zero rules. It claims 35–60%
lower energy use than MEA-based systems through waste-heat regeneration, 82–90% capture rates and a ~36-month payback.
Captured CO2 is offloaded and sold to industry (e-fuels, F&B, greenhouses), with a share returned to the shipowner.
Operating highlights (as of November 2026)
Prototype | SENU I tested on a 2.2L diesel engine at Mansoura University Energy Lab; captured 30 g CO2 from exhaust
Current phase | 4-stage onland MVP; shipowner negotiations under NDA
Next milestones | Class society Approval in Principle; first onboard pilot and sea trials (Q4 2027)
Addressable fleet | 34,000 vessels >5,000 GT must comply; ~7,000 use the Suez Canal
Recognition | 12 national & international awards since 2022 (ASRT, ASU / USAID, ISF, Abou Hashima Foundation, MSMEDA)
