Company overview
Blaze is an AI-powered, education-first investing app for first-time investors across Millennials, Gen Z and Gen Alpha.
Users learn through Duolingo-style gamified modules, build personalised Investment Playlists by goal, risk, sector and theme,
explore charts and discuss ideas with the Blaze AI chatbot. Premium unlocks personalised playlists, analytics and AI guidance.
The pre-seed funds the FRA licence that allows trade execution through Blaze, unlocking commission revenue.
