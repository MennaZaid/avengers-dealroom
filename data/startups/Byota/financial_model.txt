Company overview
Byota offers an at-home gut microbiome assessment combined with a bilingual app for meal and symptom tracking and a
practitioner portal. Nutritionists refer eligible clients, review the released report alongside food and symptom trends,
and publish plan updates between visits. Guidance is built around familiar Egyptian foods. The seed round funds Byota's own
licensed lab so testing, analysis, reporting and follow-up stay in-house.
