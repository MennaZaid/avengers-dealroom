Zaat Work · credits and top-ups
A plan covers a normal month. Busy months 
buy credits.
A credit One cent of model work. A web search is 3, a form-filling step 5.
Founder 2,000 credits a month. A typical month uses about 1,219.
Top-ups 1,000 for $12, 5,000 for $50, kept for a year. Agents stop and ask at a cap.
Expected 1 in 4 companies tops up in a month: about $4.90 each, costing us $3.60.
Top-ups are priced close to cost today. At $15 for 1,000 they would keep a third: one of the changes we’re weighing.
