A Struggling Market Is the Opportunity
Why a securitization exchange works precisely when the primary market cools
What is happening
-15%
Top-10 developer unit sales, Q1 2026 vs Q1 
2025. Contracted value down 6.5% to EGP 
271B ($5.4B).
55% to <20%
Share of buyers purchasing for investment, 2024 
to 2025. Investors have left the primary market.
24%+
Consumer mortgage rates. Roughly 115K 
mortgage clients in a country of 110M. 
Developers fund construction from instalment 
sales.
What it means for Nvest
Supply
Developers financed by pre-sales need debt-free capital 
when sales slow. Securitization is the alternative to 24% 
bank debt.
Demand
Investors did not lose interest in property; they lost the 
ability to enter at EGP 500K and to exit at all. EGP 50 
and a buyback facility bring them back.
Pricing
A correcting market rewards whoever can price assets 
fairly and fast. IPVI does that at 87% agreement with 
licensed appraisers.
Our revenue is an underwriting fee on assets that need capital. A tight market turns owners into sellers.
