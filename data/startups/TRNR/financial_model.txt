Company overview
TRNR is one straight line from wanting to play a sport to playing it. Players pick a sport, compare coaches by profile, price
and open slots, chat with the coach and lock the session with an in-app deposit. The coach then books the venue in the app,
and venues fill their quiet hours. TRNR launches with tennis and run clubs in 5th Settlement and Sheikh Zayed, targeting
adults aged 18 to 35, and replaces the WhatsApp chains most people use today to find a coach.
