Company overview
Finalyze reads Arabic, scanned and legacy financial filings that machines cannot, turning them into structured, source-linked
financial statements. Every figure is cross-checked across years and statements and linked to its source page, with an
analyst reviewing anything the engine is unsure of. Data is delivered in English or Arabic via feed, app or Excel. The same
engine runs inside banks and lenders on their own servers to turn private-company documents into credit-ready data.
