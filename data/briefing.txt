01 · The competition
What is The DealRoom?
The DealRoom is a Venture Capital simulation. Your team is a VC fund. You research real 
early-stage startups, meet their founders, value each business and decide where your capital 
goes. Then you defend your portfolio in front of judges and industry professionals.
As the first AI-driven VC competition in MENA, you can build your own AI workflows to power 
your research and decisions.

04 · Questions to ask (1 of 2)
Think about the company
Not every reason to invest fits in a spreadsheet. Weigh the qualitative case as seriously as 
the numbers.

Team & founders
Why is this team the one to win 
this market?
What did you learn from them at 
the Open Day?
Can a solo founder carry it, or 
who is missing?
Have they executed before?

Market & competition
How big is the real market in 
Egypt, and beyond?
Who else solves this problem, and 
what’s the edge?
How do regulation and timing help 
or hurt?
Why now?

Traction & product
What is proven, and what is still 
a promise?
Paying customers, pilots or 
a waitlist?
What has to be true for this 
to work?
What is the biggest single risk?

=== p9
06 · Your pitch deck
Don’t pitch the startups. 
Pitch your decisions.
The judges already know every detail of all 13 startups. Your deck needs no slides explaining 
what a startup does. Spend your time on why you invested.
SKIP
“We chose Startup X because it does 1, 2 and 3.”
SAY
“We put 20% in Startup X because of the team, 
the price and the return we expect by year 5.”

=== p11
08 · The most important slide
There is no right answer.
No portfolio wins because it holds X, Y and Z. Judges score how you got there: your 
reasoning, your evidence, your judgment and your process. Two teams can back 
completely different startups and both score top marks.
Think creatively. Build your own method. Own your choices.

10 · Working with AI
AI is optional. 
Your investment case is not.
You don’t have to build AI workflows or use Skills. They can help you work faster and dig 
deeper, so your work is better. But you are judged on your presentation and how you invest, 
not on how you used AI.
OPTIONAL
AI workflows, Skills, Research, AI-built models
WHAT YOU’RE JUDGED ON
Your deck, your portfolio and weights, your 5-year 
forecast and your reasoning

Six ways to put Claude to work

A Project as your data room

decks, financial models and your Open 
Day notes. Every chat starts with the 
full context.

Research for market

cited report back. Check the sources.

Build the models

valuation, dilution across rounds and
