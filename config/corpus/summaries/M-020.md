# Jobs to Be Done (team summary)

Source: Christensen, Hall, Dillon and Duncan, "Know Your Customers' Jobs to Be Done", Harvard Business Review
(2016): https://hbr.org/2016/09/know-your-customers-jobs-to-be-done. All rights reserved; our own summary.

Idea: customers "hire" a product to get a job done in a particular circumstance. Understanding the job (the
progress the person is trying to make, with its functional, social and emotional sides) explains why people
buy and switch better than demographics do. Competitors are whatever else people hire for the same job,
including spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, paper, or doing nothing.

How to find jobs: ask about the moment someone last switched to or from a solution: what was happening, what
they tried, what made them choose. Struggles and workarounds are signals of an under-served job.

How Venture OS uses it: the opportunity finder states each opportunity as a job in a circumstance; the market
definer lists substitutes (non-software ways of doing the job) next to direct competitors.
