# Evaluating startup ideas, YC Startup School (team summary)

Source: Jared Friedman, "How to Get Startup Ideas" (Y Combinator):
https://www.ycombinator.com/library/8g-how-to-get-startup-ideas
Licence: UNVERIFIED. Our own summary; short quotes only, with the link.

Questions YC suggests asking of any idea, which we turn into scoring criteria:
- Founder–market fit: are these founders well placed to build this?
- How big is the market (now, or growing fast)?
- How acute is the problem? Do people really need it now?
- Is there competition? Competition often means there is a real market; the question is what you can do better.
- Do you want this yourself, or know people who do?
- Did this recently become possible (new technology, regulation, behaviour)?
- Are there good proxies (a similar company that worked elsewhere)?
- Is it a good idea to work on for years?
- Is it scalable, and is there a path to people paying?
Common mistakes: solutions in search of a problem, ideas that are hard to get started on, and "tarpit" ideas
that many have tried and failed for structural reasons.

How Venture OS uses it: the idea scorer's criteria (pain, people already paying, competition, founder fit,
budget/time fit, reachable market) follow these questions; saturated "tarpit" markets with strong free
alternatives push the gate towards STOP.
