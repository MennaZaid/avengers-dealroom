# Business Model Canvas (team summary)

Source: Osterwalder and Pigneur, *Business Model Generation* (Strategyzer): https://www.strategyzer.com
Book: all rights reserved. Canvas licence: UNVERIFIED (believed CC BY-SA 3.0). Our own summary.

The Business Model Canvas describes how a business creates, delivers and captures value in nine blocks:
customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key
activities, key partnerships and cost structure. The right side is about customers and value; the left side is
about the operations that make it possible.

How to use it well: one canvas per business model idea; keep each block short; mark what is known (with a
source) and what is assumed; compare alternative canvases instead of polishing one.

How Venture OS uses it: the idea scorer compares the starting idea with alternatives using the same building
blocks (who pays, what for, through which channel, at what cost to deliver); for digital products the "key
resources" and "cost structure" blocks are checked against the founder's skills, budget and time.
