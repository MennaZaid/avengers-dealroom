# Value Proposition Canvas (team summary)

Source: Osterwalder et al., *Value Proposition Design* (Strategyzer):
https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas
Book: all rights reserved. Canvas licence: UNVERIFIED. Our own summary.

The Value Proposition Canvas zooms into two blocks of the Business Model Canvas. The customer profile lists the
customer's jobs (what they are trying to get done), pains (what annoys them or blocks them, and the risks they
fear) and gains (outcomes they want). The value map lists the products and services, the pain relievers and the
gain creators. Fit means the value map addresses the jobs, pains and gains that matter most to the customer.

Rules: start with the customer profile, from evidence (interviews, reviews, forum posts), not from the product;
rank pains by how severe and how frequent they are; a pain reliever must name the pain it relieves.

How Venture OS uses it: the opportunity finder writes each opportunity as a job + pain with verbatim quotes and
URLs; the idea scorer's "evidence of pain" criterion needs those quotes.
