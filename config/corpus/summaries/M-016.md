# Lean Canvas (team summary)

Source: Ash Maurya, *Running Lean* and the Lean Canvas (LEANSTACK): https://leanstack.com/lean-canvas
Licence of the canvas: UNVERIFIED (believed CC BY-SA). This is our own summary, not text from the book.

The Lean Canvas is a one-page business model for a new product, adapted from the Business Model Canvas. It puts
the riskiest parts first: the problem, the customer segment and the unfair advantage.

Boxes, in the order we fill them: 1 customer segments (and early adopters), 2 problem (top three) and existing
alternatives, 3 unique value proposition (one clear sentence), 4 solution (the smallest thing that addresses the
top problems), 5 channels (paths to customers), 6 revenue streams (price and model), 7 cost structure, 8 key
metrics (the few numbers that show progress), 9 unfair advantage (something that cannot easily be copied).

Principles: treat every box as a hypothesis to test, not a fact; test the riskiest assumption first; write the
problem as the customer would say it; list the existing alternatives customers use today, since "nothing" is
rarely true.

How Venture OS uses it: Discover fills the problem, existing alternatives and customer segment boxes with sourced
evidence (or ASSUMPTION labels); the Validate phase builds the full canvas from them.
