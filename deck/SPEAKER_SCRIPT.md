# Solo pitch script (5 minutes, about 30 seconds per slide)

1. **Title.** "We are Avengers Capital. We had $1M and 13 startups. We made five bets with one rule: proof before promise."
2. **Thesis.** Read the quote. "Revenue, contracts or a licence beat projections. And we size every cheque for the downside."
3. **Process.** "We read every deck, video and model, and scored seven factors. We checked 67 founder claims word for word and researched the market. The bar was 3 out of 5. But the final cheques are our decisions."
4. **Allocation.** "$850K across five companies, $150K in reserve. Modeeri and IGNOT get the largest cheques because they have the most proof."
5. **Why each bet.** One line each: Modeeri has contracted revenue; IGNOT is in the regulator's sandbox; Finalyze has a data moat; REE runs in 3 countries on $55K; Blaze is a small option pending FRA approval.
6. **Passes.** "Discipline matters as much as picks. Byota and Carbonless ask more than our whole fund. Motion+ is profitable but too small for VC returns. Hylix is on our watch list."
7. **Year 5.** "After dilution (Carta medians: we keep 66%), the base case is about $6.2M, 6.2x. The downside is 0.35x. The revenue cuts and downside cases are our assumptions, and the multiples are sourced."
8. **Power law.** "IGNOT alone could return the fund almost three times. That is how venture works: one winner pays for the rest."
9. **Founders.** Say it honestly: "We saw every team at the Open Day but had no one-on-ones, so we judged founders on what they have done."
10. **Overrides.** "Three places where we overruled our own model: Blaze kept small, IGNOT's price accepted but capped, reserve raised to $150K."
11. **Risks.** Name the regulation and concentration risks, and our answer to each.
12. **Close.** "$850K into five companies with proof, $150K held for the winners. Thank you."

## Likely questions and answers
- **"Why so concentrated?"** Both have the strongest evidence, and the reserve and three other bets balance them.
- **"Your valuation of IGNOT is below their cap. Why pay it?"** A regulatory sandbox and 500+ funded investors are rare. We accepted the price but capped our cheque below Modeeri's.
- **"Where do the year-5 numbers come from?"** Their own revenue plans, cut down by us, times a sector multiple from published sources: L40 for SaaS, Fortlane for proptech, Fawry on EGX for fintech.
- **"What if IGNOT and Blaze both fail?"** That is our downside case: 0.35x. The revenue companies and the reserve keep part of the fund.
- **"REE's ask?"** They didn't state one. We sized $100K to cover the cash low in their own model, about -$69K, plus a buffer.
- **"Why not Hylix at 3.0?"** No revenue and no stated ask. It is on our watch list; the reserve can follow in later.
- **"How did you use AI?"** To read and check everything. Every number is traceable to a source or labelled as an assumption. Decisions were ours.
- **Unsure?** Say "Good question: our evidence there is thin, which is exactly why we sized that cheque small."
