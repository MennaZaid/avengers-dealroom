Thesis

What we back

We back capital-efficient startups that already have paying customers or a regulator's green light, at prices we can defend, and we keep cash in reserve for the winners.

1 · Proof over promise

Revenue, contracts or a licence beat projections.

2 · A hard-to-copy edge

Regulation, proprietary data or distribution.

3 · Size for the downside

Bigger cheques only where the evidence is strongest.

Process

How 13 became 5

Data

Read every deck, video and financial model. Scored 7 factors (team 30%, market 25%, product 15%, competition, sales, funding need, other). Valued each with the Scorecard and Berkus methods.

Verification

67 founder claims checked word for word against their own deck, video and model. Market, competitors and regulation searched on the web, with cited sources.

Judgment

Bar: score of 3.0 out of 5 or higher. Max cheque $300K. We set the final cheques and overruled the model where our judgment differed.

Result: 5 invest · 8 pass · $150K kept in reserve

Sources: startup decks, videos, models; Carta; EGX; FRA3

Why each bet

The decision behind every cheque

  • Modeeri, largest cheque. The only startup with contracted ARR ($45K+): 150 locations, 12 brands, zero churn, and a Saudi VC already in for $110K.
  • IGNOT, second. Inside the FRA regulatory sandbox, with 500+ funded investors and 8 live properties. We pay their $3.5M post-money cap, so we capped our cheque.
  • Finalyze. Live product, a clear price ($140K for 5%) and a data moat on Arabic financial filings. Buyer talks are not yet contracts, so the cheque is mid-sized.
  • REE. Revenue in 3 countries (37 courts, 11 paying operators) on only $55K raised. Very capital-efficient. No ask stated, so we sized it at $100K.
  • Blaze, a small option. $200K from angels already, but launch depends on FRA approval (Q1 2027). We take a $60K option, not $300K.

What the numbers say

Power law, protected by a reserve

6.2x

Base case. IGNOT alone returns ~2.9x the fund and Modeeri ~1.7x. One winner pays for the portfolio.

0.35x

Downside. The fund keeps the $150K reserve plus small stakes in the businesses that already have revenue.

That is why our two biggest cheques go to the two startups with the most proof: contracted revenue and a regulator's sandbox.

Founders & Open Day

What we judged about the founders

  • We saw all 13 teams present at the Open Day but held no one-on-one meetings, so we judged founders on what they have done, checked in their own materials.
  • Modeeri: sold to 12 brands with zero churn. A team that can sell.
  • IGNOT: a 4x founder who got into the FRA sandbox and built a Beltone/CIB/EY stack.
  • REE: 3 countries on $55K. The most frugal team in the room.
  • Red flags we weighed: solo founders (Zaat, Matbakh, Motion+), wording that differed between deck and video (Blaze), asks larger than our fund (Byota, Carbonless).

Judgment over the machine

Where we overruled the model

Blaze: $60K, not more

It clears the score bar, but its launch depends on a regulator. We hold a small option and decide on more after FRA approval.

IGNOT: we accept their price

Our valuation was $2.25M; their cap means $3.0M pre-money. The evidence justifies it, so we pay it but keep the cheque below Modeeri's.

Reserve: $150K, not $100K

Our rules said $100K. We kept more back because our downside case is weak; follow-ons go only to proven winners.

Tools helped us read and check. The cheques are our decisions.
