Thesis

What we back

We back capital-efficient teams in markets that are not yet crowded, and we avoid bets that depend on a regulator's approval or on beating an entrenched local leader.

1 · Open markets

Little or no direct local competition, so a small team can win the market.

2 · Capital efficiency

Teams that already did a lot with very little money.

3 · Many small options

One anchor cheque, then several smaller bets. Seven shots on goal, with $120K in reserve.

Our engine · Three Lenses

67 claims checked · 13 startups · 7 cheques

Every startup was seen through three lenses: human judgment, data analysis and agent research. Where they disagreed, the team decided and wrote down why.

Data

Read every deck, video and financial model. Scored 7 factors (team 30%, market 25%, product 15%, competition, sales, funding need, other). Valued each with the Scorecard and Berkus methods.

Agent research

67 founder claims checked word for word against their own materials. Competitors searched in Egypt first, then the region, each finding with a cited link.

Human judgment

Menna and Omar voted, then set the final cheques. We overruled the tool on 6 of the 13 startups.

Avengers Capital4
Why each bet

The decision behind every cheque

  • Modeeri, our anchor ($290K). The only startup with contracted ARR ($45K+): 150 locations, 12 brands, zero churn, a Saudi VC already in. One small local rival (beXel).
  • Hylix, our big swing ($200K). A huge AI market with no Egyptian rival and an honest, technical team. No revenue yet, so a high-risk, high-reward option.
  • Finalyze ($100K). Live product and a data edge on Arabic filings. Funded regional rivals exist, so we went smaller than the $140K they asked for.
  • REE ($100K). Revenue in 3 countries on only $55K raised: the most capital-efficient team in the room.
  • Motion+ ($80K). Already profitable, ~210 paying clients, no local competitor. A low-risk bet.
  • TRNR ($80K). Exactly what they asked for, to fund 15 months of launch; an open local market.
  • VELO ($30K). A tiny ask, working unit and paid jobs; a cheap option on a lean founder.

Founders & Open Day

What we judged about the founders

  • We saw all 13 teams present at the Open Day but held no one-on-one meetings, so we judged founders on what they have done, checked in their own materials.
  • Modeeri: sold to 12 brands with zero churn. A team that can sell.
  • REE: 3 countries on $55K. The most frugal team in the room.
  • Motion+: profitable and self-funded with ~210 paying clients.
  • Hylix: an unusually honest deck ("these are test results, not revenue"). We trust what they say.

Honest limits

What our analysis can't tell us

Four bets below our own score bar

Hylix, Motion+, TRNR and VELO were team judgment calls, so we kept them small (except Hylix, our deliberate swing).

No founder meetings

We saw the teams at the Open Day but held no one-on-ones.

Forecasts are assumptions

Year-5 values rest on the founders' plans, which we cut down, and on sector multiples. The downside cases are our own estimates.

Little Egyptian exit data

Dilution and multiples come from global benchmarks; Egyptian exits are usually smaller.
